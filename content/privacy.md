I care a lot about my own online privacy, and am therefore not willing to publish a site that infringes on other's privacy. I have taken steps to ensure that I am as respectful of your privacy as possible in developing this site.

## Cookies

This site does not use any cookies. If you really want some, you can click the cookie emoji: [:cookie:](https://orteil.dashnet.org/cookieclicker/)

The site does use a single local storage entry to store your color theme preference. This is not set unless you manually switch between light and dark modes.

## Data Collection

This site uses a self-hosted instance of [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) for anonymous analytics. No personally identifiable data is collected, and only I have access to the collected data. No information is sent to any third party. See [the GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for more detailed information.
